## Getting started / 开始使用

Open Markora Air and choose a folder or import images from Files. Choose an annotation tool, draw on the image, and review the labels and geometry before marking the image complete. Use the Dataset center for supported dataset imports, exports and local annotation backups.

打开 Markora Air，从“文件”选择文件夹或导入图片。选择标注工具后绘制对象，检查类别和位置，再将图片标记为完成。“数据集中心”提供支持格式的导入、导出及本机标注备份。

## AI-assisted annotation / AI 辅助标注

Detection runs on your device. AI results are proposals that require human review; accept, discard or refine them before using them as final annotations. Custom models must have a supported input and output structure. If a model fails to load, include its format and input/output information in your support request without sharing confidential model files.

检测在设备上运行。AI 输出为待审核候选，需要人工接受、丢弃或修正后再作为正式标注使用。自定义模型需要满足支持的输入输出结构。模型无法载入时，请提供模型格式和输入输出信息，无需发送保密模型文件。

## Free trial and full access / 免费试用与完整版

Markora Air offers a one-time 7-day full-feature trial. The trial starts when you successfully claim “7-day Trial” through Apple's purchase flow, lasts 168 hours and does not renew or automatically charge you. After expiry, annotation, AI and dataset tools require the separate lifetime purchase. Projects are retained and local backup export remains available.

Markora Air 提供一次 7 天完整功能试用。通过 Apple 购买流程成功领取“7-day Trial”后开始计时，持续 168 小时，不自动续订，也不会自动扣款。到期后，标注、AI 和数据集工具需单独买断解锁；项目保留，并可导出本机备份。

Lifetime Full Access is a one-time purchase for the iPad app. The U.S. price is US$29.99; the localized price shown by the App Store at confirmation applies. No subscription or Mac purchase is required. A Mac purchase does not automatically unlock this separate iPad app.

“Lifetime Full Access”为 iPad App 的一次性买断，美国区价格为 US$29.99，实际金额以 App Store 确认页面显示的当地价格为准。无需订阅，也无需购买 Mac 版。Mac 版购买不会自动解锁这个独立的 iPad App。

## Restore purchases / 恢复购买

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. In Markora Air, open **Purchase & Restore… → Restore purchases**. An active trial restores its original expiry time; restoring does not start a new trial. If access is still missing, check your connection and contact support with the error message.

使用原购买时的 Apple 账户登录 App Store，在 Markora Air 中打开“购买与恢复… → 恢复购买”。有效试用按原到期时间恢复，恢复操作不会重新开始 7 天。仍无法恢复时，请检查网络，并将错误提示发送给我们。

## Files and backups / 文件与备份

Images and annotations are stored locally in the app's library. Export backups to a location you control before deleting the app or changing devices. Files you choose to save to iCloud Drive or another provider are handled by that provider. Check exported annotations and class ordering before using a dataset for training.

图片和标注保存在 App 本机资料库中。删除 App 或更换设备前，请将备份导出到你管理的位置。选择保存到 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务时，相应服务负责这些文件的存储。训练前请检查导出标注和类别顺序。
