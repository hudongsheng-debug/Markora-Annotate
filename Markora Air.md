# Markora Air 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-07

## 简体中文

Markora Air 是用于 iPad 图像标注、本地 AI 辅助和数据集整理的应用。本政策说明应用处理的数据及相关选择。

### 1. 图片、标注和模型

图片、标注、AI 候选记录、界面设置、导入的模型及资料库备份保存在设备的应用存储空间。导入时复制源文件，不直接改写源图片或源数据集。AI 推理通过 Apple Vision、Core ML 和 ONNX Runtime 在设备上运行，图片和模型不会上传至开发者服务器。ONNX 遥测已关闭。

### 2. 文件导入、导出和共享

当您使用系统“文件”、共享或文件提供方（例如 iCloud Drive）时，该服务可能按其设置和隐私政策传输或同步文件。应用资料库文件可通过系统文件共享访问。请自行确认共享对象及文件内容。

### 3. 买断与免费试用

购买、恢复和交易验证由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用读取经验证的交易信息，例如商品标识、原始购买时间和撤销状态，用于判断功能访问资格。开发者不会获取您的银行卡资料。

免费 7 天试用通过 Apple 的零价格内购主动领取。应用以原始交易时间计算试用期限，在本机钥匙串记录已观察到的时间，并使用设备计时信息减少回拨时钟重复试用。试用不会自动转为付费，也不会自动扣款。正式买断是独立的一次性购买，价格以 Apple 购买确认页面为准。

### 4. 广告、分析和联系支持

应用不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK。如果您主动通过邮件联系开发者，邮件地址、问题说明及附件仅用于回复和排查问题。请只发送您有权分享的资料，避免发送与问题无关的敏感信息。Apple 和所选文件服务提供方可能按各自政策处理数据。

### 5. 数据保留和删除

您可在应用中管理资料库并导出备份。移除资料库条目时，应用可能保留本机恢复备份；移除条目不等于所有副本已立即删除。卸载应用会移除应用容器中的本机项目数据，但您已导出、共享或存储于其他服务中的副本需要在对应位置管理。系统钥匙串及 Apple 交易记录可能在重装后继续保留，用于恢复访问资格和识别已领取的试用。试用结束不会自动删除项目。

### 6. 您的选择与联系

您决定导入、导出和共享哪些文件。联系支持时，可询问已提供支持资料的处理或删除；适用法律或必要记录保留要求另有规定的除外。购买记录、退款和 Apple 账户数据由 Apple 按其流程管理。

## English

Markora Air is an iPad app for image annotation, on-device AI assistance and dataset preparation. This policy explains how it handles data and the choices available to you.

### 1. Images, annotations and models

Images, annotations, AI proposals, interface settings, imported models and library backups are stored in the app’s local storage. Imports copy source files without directly rewriting the source images or datasets. Apple Vision, Core ML and ONNX Runtime run inference on-device. Images and models are not uploaded to developer servers. ONNX telemetry is disabled.

### 2. Importing, exporting and sharing files

When you use Files, system sharing or a file provider such as iCloud Drive, that service may transfer or synchronize files according to its settings and privacy policy. Library files can be accessed through system file sharing. Check the intended recipient and file contents before sharing.

### 3. Purchases and the free trial

Apple StoreKit handles purchases, restoration and transaction verification. The app reads verified transaction information, including product identifiers, original purchase dates and revocation status, to determine access. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

You actively claim the free seven-day trial through a zero-price Apple In-App Purchase. The app calculates the trial period from the original transaction date, stores a time high-water mark in the local Keychain and uses device timing information to reduce clock-rollback abuse. The trial does not automatically convert into a paid purchase or charge you. Full access is a separate one-time purchase; Apple’s confirmation shows the final price.

### 4. Advertising, analytics and support

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. If you contact the developer by email, your email address, message and attachments are used to respond and troubleshoot. Send only material you are entitled to share, and avoid unrelated sensitive information. Apple and your chosen file providers may process data under their own policies.

### 5. Retention and deletion

You can manage your library and export backups. Removing a library entry may retain a local recovery backup; removing an entry does not necessarily erase every copy immediately. Uninstalling the app removes project data in its local app container. Copies you exported, shared or stored with another service must be managed separately. System Keychain data and Apple transaction records may persist after reinstallation to restore access and recognize a previously claimed trial. Trial expiry does not automatically delete projects.

### 6. Your choices and contact

You decide which files to import, export and share. You may contact support about the handling or deletion of support materials you provided, subject to applicable legal or necessary record-retention requirements. Apple manages purchase records, refunds and Apple Account information through its own processes.
